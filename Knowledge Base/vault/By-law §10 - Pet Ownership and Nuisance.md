# By-law §10 - Pet Ownership and Nuisance

## Summary
Establishes permissible pet limits, common area hygiene standards, and noise nuisance guidelines regarding domestic pets.

## Key Regulations

| Regulation | Limitation | Violation Action |
|---|---|---|
| Unit Pet Limit | Maximum 1 dog or 2 cats per unit | Unauthorized breeds reported to AVS |
| Common Area Leash Requirement | Must be kept on leash at all times | $100 fine for unleashed animals |
| Persistent Barking Nuisance | Continuous barking > 20 mins after 10:30 PM | Formal management warning; referral to CMC |
| Lift Etiquette | Give way to residents who request space | Use Service Lift if requested |
