# Valve V-T2-08B

## Summary
Quarter-turn ball valve controlling domestic potable water supply to Unit #08-04 and Unit #08-03 on Level 8 of Tower 2.

## Operational Instructions

| Step | Operation Details |
|---|---|
| Location | Common Corridor Riser Cupboard adjacent to Lift Lobby 2, Level 8 |
| Cupboard Access | Triangular utility key (stored at Main Guardhouse) or keypad PIN `8821#` |
| Shutoff Direction | Rotate handle 90 degrees clockwise until perpendicular to water pipe |
| Emergency Notice | Notify security immediately after isolation to log timestamp |
