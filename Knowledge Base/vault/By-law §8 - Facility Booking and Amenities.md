# By-law §8 - Facility Booking and Amenities

## Summary
Governs access times, deposit requirements, and etiquette across estate amenities including BBQ pavilions, clubhouse function rooms, and swimming pools.

## Key Regulations & Tariffs

| Facility | Operating Hours | Deposit / Booking Fee | Cancellation & Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| BBQ Pavilions (Pits 1 - 4) | Session 1: 10am-2pm / Session 2: 5pm-10pm | $50 refundable deposit ($20 usage fee) | 48hr cancellation required; $50 cleaning forfeiture for uncleaned grill |
| Clubhouse Function Room | 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM | $200 deposit ($50/hr fee) | Max 30 guests; no external sound systems |
| Swimming Pool & Jacuzzi | 7:00 AM - 10:00 PM | Complimentary for residents | Proper swimwear mandatory; no glassware in pool zone |
| Tennis Court | 7:00 AM - 10:00 PM | $10/hr peak (6pm-10pm) | Non-marking shoes only; max 2 hours per booking |
