# BMSMA Section 101

## Summary
Section 101 of the Singapore Building Maintenance and Strata Management Act (BMSMA) establishes a statutory presumption of liability for inter-floor leakage in strata-titled residential buildings.

## Key Provisions

| Provision | Legal Meaning | Enforcement Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Section 101(1) | Defect presumption | Defect in upper floor unit is presumed to have caused leakage into lower unit |
| Section 101(2) | Rebuttal burden | Upper owner must legally prove defect originated from common property or lower unit |
| Section 101(3) | Strata Titles Board | Lower owner may file dispute directly with STB for repair orders and damages |
