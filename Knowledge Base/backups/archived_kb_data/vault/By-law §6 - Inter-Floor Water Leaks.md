# By-law §6 - Inter-Floor Water Leaks

## Summary
Defines the technical escalation, isolation protocols, and legal liabilities for water seepage between vertically stacked units. Aligns with statutory obligations under Singapore legislation.

## Key Regulations & Protocols

| Protocol Step | Timeframe / Standard | Action Required | Statutory Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate Emergency Isolation | Within 15 minutes of report | Turn off local riser isolation valve | Managing Agent By-laws |
| Statutory Upper Floor Presumption | In effect immediately | Upper unit presumed liable for floor slab leakage | [[BMSMA Section 101]] |
| Joint Technical Inspection | Within 48 hours | Upper and lower unit inspection by estate plumber | BMSMA Statutory Requirement |
| Rectification Notice Period | 14 calendar days | Owner must engage BCA-licensed waterproofing contractor | Building Maintenance Act |
| Failure to Rectify Penalty | Day 15 onwards | Management enters unit to rectify; costs billed + 10% admin fee | By-law Section 6.4 |
