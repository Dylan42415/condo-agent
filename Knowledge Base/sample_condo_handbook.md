### 3.2 Noise Infractions & Financial Penalties

Acoustic sensors in common corridors and resident complaints trigger immediate security dispatched patrols.

- **First Infraction (Verbal Warning):** Security officer attends unit within 15 minutes to issue formal stop-work instruction for non-compliant hours.
- **Second Infraction (Stop Work Order & Fine):** S$500 penalty deducted from the renovation deposit; instant suspension of contractor security passes for 48 hours.
- **Unapproved Sunday/Holiday Drilling:** Immediate S$1,000 deduction from deposit, site closure, and blacklisting of contractor firm.

## 6. Inter-Floor Water Leaks & Sanitary By-laws

### 6.1 BMSMA Statutory Presumption (Section 101)

Under Section 101 of the Building Maintenance and Strata Management Act, in the event of any water seepage or leakage into a lower unit through the ceiling or floor slab:
1. The defect is legally **presumed to originate from the upper unit** unless the upper unit owner proves otherwise.
2. The owner of the upper unit is legally responsible for carrying out and bearing the cost of the requisite waterproofing repairs.

### 6.2 Inspection SLA & Resolution Escalation

| Stage | Responsible Party | Timeframe / SLA | Action Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step 1: Initial Report | Aggrieved Resident / Management Office | Within 2 hours | Complaint logged; incident ticket created; notification sent to upper unit |
| Step 2: Joint Site Inspection | Building Supervisor + Upper & Lower Owners | Within 24 hours | Non-destructive moisture meter reading and dye test conducted |
| Step 3: Repair Agreement | Upper Unit Subsidiary Proprietor | Within 7 calendar days | Appoint licensed waterproofing contractor and submit method statement |
| Step 4: Rectification Works | Appointed Contractor | Within 14 calendar days | Complete membrane repair and water ponding test (48 hours) |
| Emergency Isolation | Management Security / Term Plumber | Immediate (15 mins) | If active flooding threatens electrical risers, main valve shut-off is authorized |

## 8. Refuse Disposal, Common Corridor & Fire Safety By-laws

### 8.1 Waste Disposal Procedures

- **General Household Waste:** Must be tied securely in double plastic refuse bags before deposit into the floor rubbish chute.
- **Flammable & Sharp Waste:** Strictly forbidden to throw hot ash, burning joss paper, paint thinner, chemicals, or unshielded broken glass down chutes.
- **Bulky Items & Furniture:** Chutes must not be blocked with bulky cardboard boxes, mattresses, or timber. Bulky items must be transported to the B2 Bin Centre or disposed through Town Council / licensed waste collector.

### 8.2 Fire Escape Corridors (SCDF Compliance)

In accordance with Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) fire code regulations:
- Common corridors, lift lobbies, and staircase landings must maintain a clear, unobstructed escape path of at least **1.2 meters** at all times.
- Shoe racks, bicycles, potted plants, and strollers must not encroach into the minimum 1.2m clear path.
- Non-compliant items will receive a 48-hour Removal Notice; unclaimed items will be disposed of by estate cleaners at the owner's expense.
